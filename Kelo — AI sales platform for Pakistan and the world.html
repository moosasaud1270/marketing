Curious about

Can I pay in Pakistani rupees?

Yes. Pakistani customers can pay in PKR by bank transfer, Raast, JazzCash or Easypaisa. International customers can pay by card in USD.

Can I message customers in their own language?

Yes. Templates and automations support multiple languages, so each customer hears from you in the one they prefer.

Can I sell to clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. Send invoices in PKR, USD, AED or GBP and connect global tools like Stripe and Gmail.

Is my customer data safe?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you control who on your team can see each lead.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with 14 days free, and no credit card is required.
